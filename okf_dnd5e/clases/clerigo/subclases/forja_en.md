# Forge Domain
**Base Class:** Cleric  
**Source:** Xanathar's Guide to Everything  
**Official Unlock Level:** Level 3

## Archetype Summary
Disciples of the artisan gods, they enchant weapons and armor with lasting magic and gain absolute immunity to fire damage.
