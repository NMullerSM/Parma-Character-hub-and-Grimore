# Circle of the Moon
**Base Class:** Druid  
**Source:** PHB 2024  
**Official Unlock Level:** Level 3

## Archetype Summary
Fierce protectors of the balance who take on powerful wild forms. As a bonus action, they absorb punishment and tear into enemies.
