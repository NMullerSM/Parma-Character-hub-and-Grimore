# Circle of the Stars
**Base Class:** Druid  
**Source:** PHB 2024  
**Official Unlock Level:** Level 3

## Archetype Summary
Readers of the celestial constellations, they manifest three starry forms (Archer, Chalice, Dragon) to fire light or heal without spending resources.
