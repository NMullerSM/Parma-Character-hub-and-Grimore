# Circle of the Sea
**Base Class:** Druid  
**Source:** PHB 2024  
**Official Unlock Level:** Level 3

## Archetype Summary
They channel the fury of the ocean and the storm, surrounding themselves with a tempest of spray and waves that pushes enemies and absorbs cold and lightning damage.
