# Battle Master
**Base Class:** Fighter  
**Source:** PHB 2024  
**Official Unlock Level:** Level 3

## Archetype Summary
A consummate tactician who uses d8 Superiority Dice to perform maneuvers: knocking enemies down, disarming them, riposting, and directing their allies.
