# Champion
**Base Class:** Fighter  
**Source:** PHB 2024  
**Official Unlock Level:** Level 3

## Archetype Summary
The pinnacle of raw physical power, they score critical hits on a roll of 19 or 20, gain additional Heroic Inspiration, and regenerate in combat.
