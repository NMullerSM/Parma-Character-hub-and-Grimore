# Warrior of the Elements
**Base Class:** Monk  
**Source:** PHB 2024  
**Official Unlock Level:** Level 3

## Archetype Summary
Channels the four primordial elements into their unarmed energy, extending its reach to 15 feet (4.5 m) with fire, cold, acid, or lightning damage.
