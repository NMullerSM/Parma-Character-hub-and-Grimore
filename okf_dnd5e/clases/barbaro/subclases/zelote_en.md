# Path of the Zealot
**Base Class:** Barbarian  
**Source:** PHB 2024  
**Official Unlock Level:** Level 3

## Archetype Summary
Imbued with the fervor of a deity, the Zealot deals radiant or necrotic damage with every strike and is nearly impossible to kill while raging.
