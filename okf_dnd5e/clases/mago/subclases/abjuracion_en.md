# School of Abjuration
**Base Class:** Wizard  
**Source:** PHB 2024  
**Official Unlock Level:** Level 3

## Archetype Summary
The arcane defender par excellence, they create an Arcane Ward that absorbs damage aimed at them or their allies, and they master counterspells.
