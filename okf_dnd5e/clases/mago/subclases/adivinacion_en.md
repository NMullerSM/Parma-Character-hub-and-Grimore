# School of Divination
**Base Class:** Wizard  
**Source:** PHB 2024  
**Official Unlock Level:** Level 3

## Archetype Summary
Glimpses the fabric of the future: they roll two d20s at dawn (Portent) and substitute those results for any ally's or enemy's roll.
