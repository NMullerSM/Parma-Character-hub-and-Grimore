# Wood Elf

Wood elves have keen senses and unmatched agility, adapted to the wild forest.

## Lineage Traits
- **Fleet of Foot:** Your base walking Speed increases to 35 feet (10.5 m).
- **Mask of the Wild:** You can attempt to hide even when you are only lightly obscured by foliage, heavy rain, fog, or another natural phenomenon.
- **Fey Ancestry:** You have advantage on saving throws against the Charmed condition.
- **Elven Trance:** A Long Rest takes only 4 hours of meditation.
- **Woodland Training:** You have proficiency with the longsword, shortsword, shortbow, and longbow.
