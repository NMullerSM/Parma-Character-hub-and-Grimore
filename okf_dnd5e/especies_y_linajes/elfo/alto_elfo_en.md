# High Elf

High elves have a keen mind and a natural mastery of the most subtle arcane arts.

## Lineage Traits
- **Innate Arcane Magic:** You know one cantrip of your choice from the Wizard spell list. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for this cantrip (choose when you gain it).
- **Extra Language:** You can speak, read, and write one additional language of your choice.
- **Fey Ancestry:** You have advantage on saving throws to avoid or end the Charmed condition.
- **Elven Trance:** You don't need to sleep. A Long Rest takes only 4 hours of deep meditation.
- **Elven Weapon Training:** You have proficiency with the longsword, shortsword, shortbow, and longbow.
