# Lightfoot Halfling

Agile, affable, and able to vanish from sight in an instant behind their allies.

## Lineage Traits
- **Naturally Stealthy:** You can attempt to hide even when you are obscured only by a creature that is at least one size larger than you.
- **Halfling Luck:** When you roll a natural 1 on an attack roll, ability check, or saving throw, you can reroll the die and must use the new roll.
- **Brave:** You have advantage on saving throws to avoid or end the Frightened condition.
- **Halfling Nimbleness:** You can move through the space of any creature that is one size larger than you.
