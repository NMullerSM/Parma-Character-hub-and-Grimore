# Stout Halfling

With distant dwarven ancestry, stout halflings are more robust and resistant to toxins.

## Lineage Traits
- **Stout Resilience:** You have advantage on saving throws against poison, and you have resistance to poison damage.
- **Halfling Luck:** When you roll a natural 1 on a d20, you can reroll the die and must use the new roll.
- **Brave:** You have advantage on saving throws against the Frightened condition.
- **Halfling Nimbleness:** You can move through the space of any creature larger than you.
