# Tavern Brawler

*Origin Feat*

You gain the following benefits.

Enhanced Unarmed Strike. When you hit a creature with an unarmed strike, you can deal bludgeoning damage equal to 1d4 plus your Strength modifier instead of the usual damage of an unarmed strike.

Reroll Damage. Whenever you roll a damage die for an unarmed strike, you can reroll it if you roll a 1, and you must use the new roll.

Improvised Weapons. You have proficiency with improvised weapons.

Push. When you hit a creature with an unarmed strike as part of the Attack action on your turn, you can deal the damage and also push the target up to 5 feet (1.5 m) away from you. You can use this benefit only once per turn.
