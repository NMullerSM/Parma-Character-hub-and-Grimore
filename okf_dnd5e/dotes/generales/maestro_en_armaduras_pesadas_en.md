# Heavy Armor Master

*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+, Heavy Armor Training)*

You gain the following benefits.

Ability Score Improvement. Increase your Constitution or Strength score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

Damage Reduction. When an attack hits you while you wear heavy armor, the bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage you take from it is reduced by an amount equal to your Proficiency Bonus.
