# Spell Sniper

*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+, Spellcasting or Pact Magic feature)*

You gain the following benefits.

Ability Score Improvement. Increase your Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

Bypass Cover. Your spell attack rolls ignore half cover and three-quarters cover.

Melee Spellcasting. Being within 5 feet (1.5 m) of an enemy doesn't impose disadvantage on your spell attack rolls.

Extended Range. When you cast a spell that has a range of at least 10 feet (3 m) and requires an attack roll, you can increase the spell's range by 60 feet (18 m).
