# Inspiring Leader

*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+, Wisdom or Charisma 13+)*

You gain the following benefits.

Ability Score Improvement. Increase your Wisdom or Charisma score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

Bolstering Performance. After you finish a Short or Long Rest, you can give an inspiring performance: a speech, a song, or a dance. When you do, choose up to six allies (you can include yourself) within 30 feet (9 m) of you who witnessed the performance. Each chosen creature gains a number of temporary hit points equal to your character level plus the modifier of the ability increased by this feat.
