# Feywild Influence

*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+)*

Your exposure to the magic of the Feywild grants you the following benefits.

Ability Score Improvement. Increase your Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

Fey Magic. Choose a 1st-level spell from the Divination or Enchantment school. You always have that spell and the Misty Step spell prepared. You can cast each of these spells without expending a spell slot. Once you cast either of them this way, you can't do so again until you finish a Long Rest. You can also cast these spells using spell slots of the appropriate level that you have. The spellcasting ability for these spells is the ability increased by this feat.
