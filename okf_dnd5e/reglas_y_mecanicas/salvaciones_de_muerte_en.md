# Death Saving Throws

When you drop to 0 hit points and don't die outright, you fall Unconscious. At the start of each of your turns, roll a d20 without modifiers:
- **10 or higher:** Success. After accumulating 3 successes, you stabilize at 0 hit points.
- **9 or lower:** Failure. After accumulating 3 failures, your character dies.
- **Natural 20:** You regain 1 hit point immediately and regain consciousness.
- **Natural 1:** Counts as two immediate failures.
